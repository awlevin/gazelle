# Changelog

## July 1, 2026

### 🔥 New Features
- **Gazelle is born** — a speed-reading trainer that watches your eyes. Calibrate your webcam, and an amber mark leaps from one meaningful word to the next as your gaze does the turning. Filler words are skipped on purpose; your brain fills them in.
- **Free look mode** — no text, just a ring showing where Gazelle thinks you're looking, with a fading trail. The honest mirror for your calibration.
- **Auto pacer mode** — no camera? A timer drives the mark at your chosen words-per-minute instead.
- **Comprehension quizzes** — three questions after each built-in passage, because speed without understanding is just scrolling. Session history (speed + comprehension) is saved on your device.
- **Bring your own text** — paste anything and read it with the leap.

### 💅 Nits
- Calibration got kinder: intro cards before each pass (no more missed instructions), a mid-pass blink break, and the camera preview steps aside while the dot glides. Blink freely — blinks are detected and skipped.
- While reading, tap `space` when the mark lags your eyes: it jumps to the dotted word and retunes tracking in one stroke.
